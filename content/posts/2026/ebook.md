It is almost Halloween which means it's time for the annual coat of lacquer on my gothic Scooby Doo parody. Anything to avoid trying to write more fiction, which is impossible. I spent 15m yesterday earnestly mulling [whether to take up copperplate engraving as a hobby](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoQKVpx6mvw).

You can download it here: [https://booksparker.com/book/the-house-of-endless-mourning](https://booksparker.com/book/the-house-of-endless-mourning)

Or, once that service linkrots away, at one of these links: [kindle](/static/2026/10/the-house-of-endless-mourning-kindle.epub) [epub](/static/2026/10/the-house-of-endless-mourning.epub) [pdf](/static/2026/10/the-house-of-endless-mourning.pdf)

This year I had Claude gussy up the ebook versions and find me some public domain engravings for chapter motifs. It looks *much* better.

It's becoming interesting to have this project stretch across evolving norms for AI use--I'm very hesitant to let it touch the text given what I understand to be readers' prejudices (which I agree with!). I may be in the danger zone now that I've asked Claude for a copyedit, but I think it was worth it to catch an "abyssinal" that should have been "abyssal" (none of the story is set in Ethiopia).

I did explore hiring a human illustrator but couldn't justify a multithousand-dollar expenditure for a silly hobby side project. [The guy I liked](https://reedsy.com/freelancers/j-e-l) was busy until November anyway. I will probably shell out for a proper cover eventually--the current one is perfectly satisfactory in a spare sort of way, but I am sure a real artist could deliver something striking.

The act of sharing this is embarrassing (and repetitive, I realize!) but it's become clear that even close friends don't realize I wrote this thing. It really was impossibly hard for me in a way that tossed-off blog posts are not, so I'm proud of it even though it is, by design, very stupid.

I continue to be fond of phrases like "jentacular orrery" but realize the piece's appeal will be limited--you probably have to like two specific subgenres and not mind seeing them tweaked. It's not for everyone, or maybe not even anyone who didn't watch the exact episodes of Scooby Doo that I did during my childhood visits to my cable-having grandparents (episodes which were by then already decades out of date).

But if you are not enough of a genre fan to be amused by a bunch of carefully chosen ten-penny words (I limited myself to one "tenebrous", and struck "nacreous" on the grounds that it was too much of a fad at the time); or the idea of an inscrutable rustic speaking in dialect so obscure that most of the ACC's team names can be hidden in it, well... hey, the price is right!
